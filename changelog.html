Certification Project Tracker

Change log

v3.1.0 Oct 2026 Current

Beta. Numbering restarts at 1.0.0 when the tracker goes live.

Added

  • FAA ATD approvals: an ATD devices page for Redbird ATD QAG and LOA status, grouped BATD then AATD (FMX/MCX first, the rest alphabetical), with days to expiry, a green/yellow/red status (over 365 days, 180 to 365, under 180), a resubmit-by date 180 days before expiry, and pending submissions.
  • Pending submissions show the FAA tracking number (for example 20261006-83327) from a tracking column in the spreadsheet; the existing Submission Tracker column works too.
  • Spreadsheet import in the editor: upload FAA_Approval_Tracker.xlsx and review what's new, changed or removed before saving.
  • Device pages show each device's LOA and QAG from the Redbird knowledge base in the main panel, with Open in knowledge base, and link to related certification projects.
  • Weekly knowledge base check: finds each device's QAG/LOA article, stores copies of the documents and compares the QAG version, LOA date and expiration with the spreadsheet. Differences are flagged in the editor; the spreadsheet is never changed.
  • When the knowledge base is behind the spreadsheet: a notice in the editor, a count on ATD devices in the sidebar, and a GitHub issue (emailed) that closes on its own once the knowledge base is updated.
  • Home page summary: a card for each sidebar section listing what needs attention, each row labeled with why it's there (overdue, due this week, waiting, expiring, conditional, pending, next resubmit, regulatory updates), with an all-clear line when nothing does.
  • A FAA ATD approvals section in the sidebar, and summary bar boxes: ATD resubmit due, ATD pending, ATD under 365 days, and (editor) KB behind spreadsheet.
  • Search covers ATD devices and the text of their LOAs and QAGs.

Changed

  • The v2 change log moved to the private data repo and is shown in the editor only (footer: v2 change log). This page lists v3 changes.
  • The weekly check now runs both the regulatory check and the knowledge base check, and uses the current versions of GitHub's actions.

Fixed

  • The Regulatory library offered Expand all while it was already expanded. It now opens collapsed, with Expand all; an authority with a regulatory update still opens so the flag is visible.

v3.0.0 Oct 2026

Changed

  • The tracker's data is now private. Certifications, the regulatory library and its documents live in a private repo, and the tracker asks for an access key on first visit. Keys are issued and revoked individually.
  • New address: the tracker moved to the Certification-Tracker organization on GitHub.
  • Editor sign-in asks only for your editor key; the repo details are built in.
  • Regulatory authority is chosen from a list (EASA, FAA, Transport Canada, UK CAA, CAA-HU, HCAA), and certification level suggests standard names, so the same authority or level is always written the same way.
  • Existing data was converted: UKCAA became UK CAA, FNPTII became FNPT II, Hungary's authority became CAA-HU, and one country entry was corrected.
  • Customize is now a small link under the summary bar, and Home stays fixed at the top of the sidebar while the lists scroll.

Added

  • Qualification section on each certification: status (Pending, Conditional, Qualified, Expired, Withdrawn), certificate or LOA number, issue and expiry dates, next evaluation, qualification basis (linked to the regulatory library) and conditions. Expiry and evaluation dates within 90 days are highlighted.
  • Summary bar measures for qualifications: expiring in 90 or 180 days, expired, evaluation due in 90 days, conditional, and completed certifications with no qualification details.
  • Google Drive documents can be previewed in the main panel, with Open in Drive.
  • Weekly regulatory check: the official sources for the library documents are checked every week. An update is flagged in the sidebar, on the home page and on the document, naming the new version and comparing it with your copy.
  • "Editor" label under the title on the editor page, and a sign-out option that removes keys from the browser.
  • The full v2 history is kept in a private archive, linked as v2 revision history in the editor's footer.
